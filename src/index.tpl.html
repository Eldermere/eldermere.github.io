The world

The Age of the Falling Star

Long before the kingdoms, the Firstborn found the Heart of Creation buried beneath the world. One of them, Vaelthar, wanted to become its master. After centuries of war they sealed him under the continent and erased his name from history.

Thousands of years later, a star falls from the sky. The people of Eldermere think it is a natural phenomenon. It is a fragment of the Heart, and the seals are starting to break. The magic of Frostpeak becomes unstable, the forests of Silverwood begin to change, and beneath Blackspire something begins to breathe again.

Four powers shape the realm

The Firstborn

Creation · Knowledge · Balance

The ancient civilization that built the seals and then vanished. Their symbol still marks the ruins.

The Iron Wolves

Strength · War · Survival

Silverwood warriors led by Darius Veyne, the Black Wolf. “Peace creates weakness. War creates strength.”

The Veiled Circle

Magic · Freedom · Evolution

Forbidden mages of Frostpeak led by Seraphine Voss, the Frost Witch, who embraced the Star's power.

Vaelthar

Dominion · Unity · Eternal

The First Darkness, sealed beneath Blackspire. He offers power and peace to anyone who will kneel.
